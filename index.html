Accordion

When should I use Hammhaus?
Use it for editorial portfolios, crisp landing pages, internal tools, and prototypes that need a strong point of view.
How do I customize it?
Override the --hh-* tokens globally or inside a section, then keep using the same classes.
Does it require JavaScript?
No. The reusable stylesheet is pure CSS.
